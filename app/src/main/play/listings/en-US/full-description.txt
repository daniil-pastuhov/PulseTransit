PulseTransit shows live departures and the fastest way to where you're going, for all public transport in Norway. Take it with you and it follows where you are, or mount a spare phone or tablet by the front door as an always-on departure board.

LIVE DEPARTURES
• Real-time departures for all public transport in Norway, from Entur
• Delays, cancellations and service messages
• Pick the platforms and directions you care about, and hide lines you never take

ROUTES
• Fastest route to the places you travel to most, including walking time
• Choose the days and hours each route shows, like weekday mornings for your trip to work
• Search for any stop, address or place and get routes there
• Tap a route to see every leg: lines, platforms, live times, walks and changes
• Prefer the fastest trip, fewer transfers or less walking, pick which transport to use, and set your walking speed

ON THE SPOT
• Follows where you are: routes to your destinations from right here, and departures from the nearest stop
• Pick another stop nearby, or let it follow the nearest one
• Drag to share the screen between routes and departures, or fold either one away. It stays the way you left it

WALL BOARD
• Always-on mode that keeps the screen awake
• Wakes up when someone walks past, using the front camera. Frames are analysed on the device and never stored or sent anywhere
• Dims and switches between day and night theme from the light sensor
• Pixel shifting protects OLED screens from burn-in
• Refreshes less often at night and while dimmed to save power
• Keeps showing the last known departures and weather when the connection drops

WEATHER
• Current weather and forecast for your location, from MET Norway. Can be turned off

Departure data from Entur. Weather data from MET Norway (yr.no).
